How it started: a chair problem

Summer 2025 to summer 2026.

  1. Summer 2025

    The back pain shows up

    Grad school means long hours in the lab, and most of them sitting in a chair. By summer 2025 my lower back had started to hurt, and it kept hurting.

  2. 2025 – 2026

    I tried buying my way out of it

    Every fix helped for a little while, and then the pain came back. Hover over each one to see how it went:

    ✕ Patches ✕ Better chair ✕ Better bed ✕ Better pillow
    Four purchases, four temporary fixes.
  3. May 2026

    I started running instead

    I stopped looking for a product and tried moving more instead. I began with a few short runs in mid-May, then started running properly in the last week of the month.

  4. About one month in · late June 2026

    My lower back hurt less

    About a month after I started, I noticed my back was hurting less. That was the first thing all year that kept working, so I kept running.

What I learned

  • Equipment can't make up for not moving. The chair, bed and pillow made sitting more comfortable. They didn't change how much I sat.
  • Give it a month. It took about a month of running before I noticed my back hurting less.
  • Consistency beats big weeks. My biggest week was 56.5 km, but I'm prouder of the 13 weeks in a row above 30 km.
  • Watching the total grow keeps me going. Logging every run and seeing the next 100 km get closer is a big part of why I keep showing up.

This is what worked for my back. It isn't medical advice. If your back hurts, have someone qualified look at it before you start running.
