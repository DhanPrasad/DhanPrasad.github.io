Astronomy

Things I've pointed a telescope at

Photographs from my own sessions, with notes on how each one was captured.

Things goes right sometimes, take out telescope wit energy, compelete aligning, track the objects to look at. Simple right ? No way !!! Connection lost, a gust of wind takes out of focus, camera connection lost, redo whole thing again. And still somehow clouds starts to cover. Getting a perfect shot is not as easy as we think, it requires everything to be right. These are some of my best captures, I think so, have a look.
